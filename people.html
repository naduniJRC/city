People

The minds behind City Scales

The Team

A community of architects, educators, and researchers building bridges between cultures through collaborative design inquiry.

City Scales is driven by a small, dedicated team of academics committed to expanding the boundaries of international architectural education. Each member brings deep expertise in their regional urban context.

Co-Founder & Director

Dr. Nalaka Jayaweera

University of Moratuwa · Sri Lanka

Senior Lecturer in the Department of Architecture at the University of Moratuwa. His research focuses on urban morphology, tropical urbanism, and community-based design processes. Workshop host for the inaugural Colombo edition.

AS
Co-Founder & Director

Adrià Sánchez

La Salle – URL Barcelona · Spain

Associate Professor at La Salle Campus Barcelona, Universitat Ramon Llull. His practice and research engage with Mediterranean urban scales, public space, and housing typologies across southern European cities.

Co-Founder & Director

José Remón

XJTLU · China

Associate Professor in the Department of Architecture Design at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University. His work explores East Asian urban transformations, high-density environments, and cross-cultural design methodologies.

Japan Coordinator

Prof. Hiroshi Tanaka

University of Tsukuba · Japan

Professor in the Faculty of Art and Design at the University of Tsukuba. Specialises in Japanese urban infrastructure, research city planning, and the spatial logic of post-industrial urban districts.

VT
Patagonia Coordinator

Dr. Valentina Torres

Universidad San Sebastián · Chile

Associate Professor in the School of Architecture at Universidad San Sebastián, Patagonia campus. Her research investigates extreme landscapes, sustainable construction in remote territories, and bioclimatic design strategies.

MR
Collaborator

Marco Rossini

Università Iuav di Venezia · Italy

Researcher and architect based in Venice. His work bridges historical urban analysis and contemporary intervention strategies, focusing on water cities, infrastructure resilience, and heritage-sensitive urban renewal.

Marco has been instrumental in developing the conceptual framework for the upcoming Venezia edition, drawing on his extensive knowledge of the city's unique water urbanism and its challenges of adaptation and preservation over centuries.
